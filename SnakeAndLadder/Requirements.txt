Design a snake and ladder game.

Requirements:
Entities:
    1. The board should be ideally 100 size(it should be configurable).
    2. The dice should be there, ideally 6 size(but can be configurable also).
    3. Entities: Board, dice, snakes & ladders, users
    4. Multiple users and multiple snakes & ladders should be supported.
Game rules:
    1. Snakes and ladder should be unique.
    2. Two users can't stay on same place.
    3. Thrice 6 is not allowed, the third time should be neglected.
    4. When game is about to complete, we need to check full completion
        i.e. is user is at 96, they would need exact 4 to proceed,
        anything greater than that would be ignored.

Non-Functional requirements:
    1. Rules should be extendable.
